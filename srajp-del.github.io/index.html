A bit about me

Welcome to my website

My name is Shirin, and I am a passionate and dedicated Master’s student in Bioelectrical Engineering at K. N. Toosi University of Technology. With a keen interest in the intersection of biomedical and computer science research, I am driven by the pursuit of innovative solutions that bridge the gap between these two disciplines.

Throughout my academic journey, I have been captivated by the potential of cutting-edge technologies in transforming healthcare and improving the quality of life for individuals worldwide.

I am currently working on modeling the structural covariance network development of the infant auditory cortex using magnetic resonance imaging to figure out the pattern of healthy development.

  • Infant auditory cortex & SCN development
  • Medical imaging & MRI analysis
  • Healthcare technology & clinical insight
